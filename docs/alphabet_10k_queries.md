# Alphabet 10-K Retrieval Questions by Metric

## Gross Margin / Gross Profit Margin
How profitable is Alphabet's core business after production costs in 2024? Calculate the gross profit margin using revenue and cost of revenues.

## Operating Margin
How efficiently is Alphabet converting revenue into operating profit in 2024? Analyze the operating margin using income from operations and total revenue.

## Net Margin / Net Profit Margin
What percentage of Alphabet's 2024 revenue ultimately becomes profit? Calculate the net profit margin using net income and total revenue.

## ROE (Return on Equity)
How effectively did Alphabet generate returns for its shareholders in 2024? Analyze the return on equity using net income and total stockholders' equity.

## ROA (Return on Assets)
How well did Alphabet utilize its total asset base to generate profit in 2024? Calculate the return on assets using net income and total assets.

## Current Ratio
Does Alphabet have enough short-term assets to cover its short-term obligations in 2024? Analyze the current ratio using total current assets and total current liabilities.

## Quick Ratio
Can Alphabet meet its short-term liabilities without relying on inventory in 2024? Calculate the quick ratio using current assets, inventories, and current liabilities.

## Cash Ratio
How much of Alphabet's short-term liabilities can be covered by cash alone in 2024? Analyze the cash ratio using cash and cash equivalents against total current liabilities.

## Debt-to-Equity Ratio
How leveraged is Alphabet's balance sheet in 2024? Calculate the debt-to-equity ratio using total debt (long-term and short-term) and total stockholders' equity.

## Interest Coverage
Did Alphabet comfortably cover its interest expenses with its operating income in 2024? Analyze the interest coverage ratio using income from operations and interest expense.

## Inventory Turnover
How efficiently did Alphabet cycle through its inventory in 2024? Calculate the inventory turnover ratio using cost of revenues and average inventory (beginning and ending balances).

## Asset Turnover
How effectively did Alphabet use its assets to generate revenue in 2024? Analyze the asset turnover ratio using total revenue and average total assets (beginning and ending balances).

## P/E Ratio
How is the market valuing Alphabet's earnings in 2024? Calculate the price-to-earnings ratio using the year-end stock price and earnings per share.

## EV/EBITDA
What is Alphabet's valuation relative to its operating earnings in 2024? Analyze the EV/EBITDA using income from operations, depreciation and amortization, and total revenue. //

## Revenue Growth (YoY)
How fast is Alphabet growing its top line? Calculate the year-over-year revenue growth rate using total revenue for 2024 and 2023.

## Free Cash Flow
How much cash did Alphabet generate after capital investments in 2024? Calculate free cash flow using net cash from operating activities and capital expenditures (purchases of property and equipment).

## Net CapEx
What is Alphabet's net investment in fixed assets after accounting for depreciation in 2024? Calculate net CapEx using ending PP&E, beginning PP&E, and depreciation and amortization expense.
